Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

ReadFlo ("the app") is an AI-powered text-to-speech reader developed by Mariam Azam. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how it is protected. By using ReadFlo, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information the App Handles

Content you provide (stored on your device):

  • Documents and text you import (PDF, DOCX, TXT, EPUB), paste, or scan with your camera (OCR).
  • Web articles you import via a URL you provide.
  • Your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, favorites, listening history, and app preferences.

This content is stored locally on your device by default and is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes this information permanently.

Information processed to provide features:

  • Text you ask the app to read may be processed by on-device or third-party text-to-speech services to generate audio. Saved audio exports are generated through Google's text-to-speech service at your request.
  • Scanned images are processed for optical character recognition (OCR) to extract text.
  • Documents you summarize are processed by cloud AI services to generate a summary.
  • Word lookups use a public dictionary API, and translations use Google Translate, only when you use those features.

Automatically collected diagnostics:

  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics, used solely to identify and fix bugs.
  • Anonymous usage analytics via Firebase Analytics (e.g., which features are used), used to improve the app. No document content, no names, and no contact details are included in analytics.

3. Accounts & Purchases

ReadFlo does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play (and Apple App Store on iOS) through RevenueCat, which validates purchases and manages subscription status. Payment details are handled by the store platform and are never collected or stored by us.

4. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat (purchase validation) — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Translate / text-to-speech endpoints, used only when you use translation or audio export features.

5. Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app's features, and only under their own privacy commitments.

6. Data Security

Your content stays on your device under your control. Network requests to third-party services use encrypted connections (HTTPS). We take reasonable technical measures to protect information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • Delete any imported document, summary, or history entry at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions anytime from your Google Play (or App Store) account settings.
  • To request deletion of any server-side data (e.g., analytics or purchase records held by service providers), contact us at the email below.

8. Children

ReadFlo is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at mariamazam1788@gmail.com. See also our dedicated Data Deletion page below.

Data Deletion

App: ReadFlo  •  Developer: Mariam Azam  •  Contact: mariamazam1788@gmail.com

What data ReadFlo handles

  • On your device: imported documents, pasted text, summaries, highlights, favorites, listening history and app preferences. This data is stored locally on your phone or tablet and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Crash logs: collected by Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose and fix bugs. No document content or personal details are included.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous feature-usage data via Firebase Analytics.
  • Purchase records: created and retained by Google Play under your Google account.

Steps to request deletion of your data

  • Step 1 — Delete on-device data: delete individual documents or history from within the app, or simply uninstall the app. This removes all locally stored content immediately and permanently. We cannot recover it, and no copy exists on our servers.
  • Step 2 — Request server-side deletion: email mariamazam1788@gmail.com with the subject line "ReadFlo data deletion request" and include the approximate period you used the app. We will delete your associated analytics and crash-log records and confirm by email.
  • Step 3 — Purchases: subscription and purchase records are managed by Google Play. View, cancel or remove them in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. We cannot delete Google's purchase records on your behalf.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: Firebase Analytics data and Crashlytics records associated with your usage.
  • Deleted automatically: crash logs within approximately 90 days; all on-device data the moment the app is uninstalled.
  • Retained by third parties: purchase/subscription records by Google Play (under Google's own policies), and aggregated, anonymised analytics that cannot identify you.

Retention period

On-device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Crash logs are kept for approximately 90 days. Analytics data is kept for a maximum of 14 months. Deletion requests are completed and confirmed by email within 30 days.
